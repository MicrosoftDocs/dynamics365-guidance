# Overview of the Rent to return process areas

***Applies to: Dynamics 365 Finance, Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management***

The *Rent to return* end-to-end process is broken into six business process areas with 25 processes, and 59 scenarios. The six process areas are:

- *Define rental strategy and policies*
- *Initiate rental agreements*
- *Fulfill rental agreements*
- *Manage active rentals*
- *Return rental equipment*
- *Analyze rental performance*

This end-to-end process is new with the October 2026 update of the business process catalog, and content to explain the end-to-end process will come later.
