# The Operate phase in Success by Design

The **Operate** phase is the last of five stages in the Success by Design framework.  This article provides a high-level introduction to the phase in the context of Success by Design.

[!INCLUDE [impl-guide-sdb-operate](../includes/impl-guide-sdb-operate.md)]

This phase has three main sections.

## Service the solution

Most implementation projects focus on building and deploying the solution. What comes next is often treated like an afterthought. The articles in the *Service the solution* section help you plan for keeping your solution optimal and healthy, including monitoring system health, applying service updates, and maintaining your environment.  

Learn more at [Monitor and service the health of your Dynamics 365 solution implementation](service-solution.md).

## Transition to support

A solution is something that your users rely on and that requires ongoing support. Providing support requires a support strategy. The articles in the *Transition to support* section help you prepare, design, and run a support model for your solution.

Learn more at [Create a support strategy for your Dynamics 365 solution](transition-to-support.md).

## Data management

Data is everywhere, and it's one of the most valuable assets of any business. With the right data, you can make informed decisions, improve customer engagement, and gather real-time information about your products in the field.

Learn more at [Manage your data in Dynamics 365 implementation projects](data-management.md).
