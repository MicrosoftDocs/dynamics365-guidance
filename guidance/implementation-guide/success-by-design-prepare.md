# The Prepare phase in Success by Design

The **Prepare** phase is the fourth of five stages in the Success by Design framework. This article provides a high-level introduction to the phase in the context of Success by Design.  

[!INCLUDE [impl-guide-sdb-prepare](../includes/impl-guide-sdb-prepare.md)]

This phase has two main sections.

## Prepare for go-live

Go-live is a critical milestone in the deployment of a business solution to production. It's when you test and validate that everything works as expected, not just on their own, but as a whole. After a successful go-live, the new solution is operational.

Learn more at [Prepare to go live](prepare-to-go-live.md).

## Training strategy

The articles in the *Training strategy* section outline the approach to ensure the readiness of every person who is involved in your implementation. The articles review high-level objectives, identify risks that might arise during implementation, and share best practices for you to keep in mind as you develop your own training plan.

Learn more at [Develop your own training strategy for your Dynamics 365 implementations](training-strategy.md).
