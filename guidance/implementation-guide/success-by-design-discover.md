# The Discover phase in Success by Design

The **Discover** phase is the first of five stages in the Success by Design framework. This article provides a high-level introduction to the phase in the context of Success by Design.  

[!INCLUDE [impl-guide-sdb-discover](../includes/impl-guide-sdb-discover.md)]

This phase has three main sections.

## Implement cloud solutions

The articles in the *Implement cloud solutions* section help you plan and execute your cloud implementation. Find guidance for how to upgrade from on-premises to online, customize and extend cloud apps, and operate in the evergreen cloud with Dynamics 365. You also discover why you need to adopt a cloud mindset for a successful digital transformation.

Learn more at [Implement cloud solutions with Dynamics 365](implementing-cloud-solutions.md).

## Drive app value

The articles in the *Drive app value* section outline the approach to digital transformation and discuss strategies for shortening the time to value, cutting through the noise, and focusing on business outcomes with Dynamics 365.

Learn more at [Drive long-term business application value through digital transformation](drive-app-value.md).

## Implementation strategy

If you're embarking on a digital transformation journey, implementing business applications is a key milestone for your success. To turn your vision into reality, you need an implementation strategy that acts as a framework for planning and delivering predictable outcomes. An implementation strategy defines the strategic choices and decisions you make when you design, test, deploy, and operate Dynamics 365 applications.

Learn more at [Plan an implementation strategy](implementation-strategy.md).
