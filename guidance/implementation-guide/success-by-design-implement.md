# The Implement phase in Success by Design

The **Implement** phase is the third of five stages in the Success by Design framework. This article provides a high-level introduction to the phase in the context of Success by Design.  

[!INCLUDE [impl-guide-sdb-implement](../includes/impl-guide-sdb-implement.md)]

This phase has five main sections.

## Application lifecycle management

Use application lifecycle management (ALM) to turn ideas into reality and make it work well for your customers. ALM is a way of managing your solution from conception to operation.

Learn more at [Bring successful solutions to customers with application lifecycle management (ALM) strategies](application-lifecycle-management.md).

## Performance

When you deliver a new Dynamics 365 implementation, users typically expect an improvement in system performance. Although Dynamics 365 products are scalable and powerful, achieving a high-performance solution depends on many factors, not just the platform, apps, and services that make up your solution. 

Learn more at [Explore how Dynamics 365 products and tools can improve performance](performing-solution.md).

## Extend the solution

Dynamics 365 apps offer rich capabilities out of the box, but sometimes you must customize them to fit your specific business processes and requirements. However, extending a cloud solution can have implications for its usability, accessibility, performance, security, compliance, scalability, maintainability, supportability, and future-proofing. You want to strike the right balance between adding value and avoiding complexity, cost, and risk.

Learn more at [Extend Dynamics 365 apps without compromising performance or security](extend-your-solution.md).

## Integrate with other solutions

Dynamics 365 apps offer a rich and mature set of features for various industries. But sometimes you might want to extend your processes or use data from other sources.  

Learn more at [Integrate your Dynamics 365 apps with other solutions](integrate-other-solutions.md).

## Testing strategy

When you implement a Dynamics 365 solution, you want it to meet your business needs and run smoothly. To achieve this goal, test your solution thoroughly before you deploy it to production. Testing helps you avoid problems that could delay adoption, damage your reputation, and reduce confidence in the solution.

Learn more at [Test your Dynamics 365 solution before deployment](testing-strategy.md).
